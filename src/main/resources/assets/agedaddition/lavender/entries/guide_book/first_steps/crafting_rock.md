To craft your first [flint tools](^agedaddition:first_steps/equipment) & other items, you need a crafting rock.


Gather **rocks** & **flint** from the ground and break **leaves** to gather **sticks**.


Then just combine 2 rocks in your inventory crafting to get a crafting rock.
